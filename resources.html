Model UN vocabulary

Amendment
A proposed change to a draft resolution. Friendly if every sponsor agrees to it, unfriendly if it goes to a vote.
Bloc
A group of delegates aligned on a topic, often drafting a resolution together.
Dais
The chair and staff running committee from the front of the room.
Delegate
A student representing a country or entity in committee.
Gavel
The tool the chair uses to open and close sessions and call the room to order.
Moderated caucus
A structured speaking format with a set topic, per-speaker time, and total duration.
Placard
The nameplate a delegate raises to be recognized and speak.
Position paper
A one- to two-page brief on a country's stance, written before committee begins.
Quorum
The minimum attendance required for committee to conduct business.
Rapporteur
The dais member who tracks the speakers' list and committee paperwork.
Signatory
A delegate who wants a resolution introduced for debate, without necessarily supporting it.
Simple majority
Half of those present and voting, plus one.
Sponsor
A delegate who helped write a draft resolution.
Unmoderated caucus
Open time for delegates to negotiate directly and draft text together.
Working paper
An early, informal draft, before it's introduced to committee as a resolution.
Yield
Passing remaining speaking time to another delegate, to questions, or back to the chair.
