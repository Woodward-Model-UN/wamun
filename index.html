Delegates in training, diplomats in practice.

Model UN at Woodward puts students in the seat of a country they've researched, inside a simulated UN committee. The club competes at an Atlanta-area conference and an out-of-state conference each year, and runs its own on-campus conference, WAMUN.

See WAMUN XI Join the club

Three ways the club runs

Country research

Every delegate studies a country's foreign policy before committee, then argues its positions, drafts resolutions, and negotiates on the floor.

Away conferences

The club sends delegations to an Atlanta-area conference and an out-of-state conference each year, competing against other high school teams.

WAMUN, on campus

Woodward runs its own annual conference, WAMUN, staffed and chaired by our own Secretariat and Cabinet.
